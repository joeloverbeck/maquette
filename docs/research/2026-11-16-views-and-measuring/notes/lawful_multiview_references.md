# Lawful multi-view references: where official head drawings are published

Researched 2026-11-16. Marks: **[P]** I opened and read the owning page; **[P, seen]** I also looked at the rendered drawing in a browser (reduced screenshots, nothing saved); **[S]** search summary or retelling; **Inference** is my own reasoning. My look at a drawing is a diagnostic, not acceptance. Rules read: `EVID-1`, `EVID-2` (revision 2026-11-15.1).

Decision served: whether measuring a first table in three views can start now, and at what cost to the owner. Appetite used: 22 searches and 36 page loads (five refused, missing or empty). Background only, not legal advice.

## Summary

Lawful free supply exists but is uneven across the three views: rights holders publish level, neutral front heads freely and often, while profile heads of the same character appear only on some official character pages and in the free samples of official setting-material books. Three-quarter heads are everywhere, but in every sheet I looked at they carried an expression or a dip of the head, and free profile heads were limited to lead characters; the one adult woman I checked had no head sheet at all. No generic search surfaced these pages: each was found by going to a named work's official character page or its book's sample, so supply is found work by work, and the evidence is seven sources seen on one day.

| View | Free official supply | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Front | Plentiful | A level, neutral front head or bust in 5 of 5 sources that showed heads (E1, E3, E4, E5, E6) |
| Three-quarter | Plentiful in count, thin as level and neutral | Present in 5 of 5; with an expression or tilt in 4, and a small head on a full-body figure in the fifth (E3) |
| Side | Thin on the free web; routine in books | Profile head in 1 of 2 official sites (lead only), 0 of 1 press sheets, 2 of 2 book samples that reached character pages |

All three views of one character on one page or sheet: E1, E5, E6 (3 of 7).

## 1. Where rights holders publish model sheets

Checked examples:

- **E1 [P, seen]** <https://gundam-official.com/witch-from-mercury/character/2/>. Rights holder's portal, 2022–23 television series, a lead (teenage girl). Coloured full-body figures labelled FRONT and BACK, then a CLOSE UP block of three heads: front (level, neutral), left profile (level, neutral), three-quarter (mouth open, head dipped).
- **E2 [P, seen]** <https://gundam-official.com/witch-from-mercury/character/29/>. Same site, a supporting adult woman: FRONT and BACK full-body only, no close-up heads. The index page shows about sixty coloured busts, front or slightly turned, some with expressions, adult women among them.
- **E3 [P, seen]** <https://frieren-anime.jp/character/>. Official site of a 2023 series: a grid of front-facing coloured busts, adults included. A character page (`/character/chara_group1/1-5/`) shows one coloured full-body figure in three-quarter with a costume toggle; no head sheet, no profile.
- **E4 [P, seen]** <https://av.watch.impress.co.jp/docs/news/1556621.html> (image 2 of 4). Press relay of a studio-released coloured sheet for a 2024 season, a teenage girl: full-body front and three heads, one front and neutral, two three-quarter (laughing, surprised). No profile. Credited to the rights holders.
- **E5 [P, seen]** <https://bookwalker.jp/de770f90d7-d286-48d8-aa66-f5391fe552b1/>, the publisher's free sample linked from <https://www.kadokawa.co.jp/product/321711001043/>. Official setting-material book of a 2017 series. Character pages start at the fourth spread: a 全身 block (back, left profile, front, front without coat) and a 表情 block of about ten heads (left profile, front neutral, front smiling, three-quarter with expressions, head dipped, rear three-quarter). Coloured; child characters with very large heads.
- **E6 [P, seen]** <https://animestyle.jp/shop/archives/3472>, sample spread pp. 102–103. Line-art sheets of a 2024–25 series: a boy's full-body row (front, three-quarter, profile with a small head inset, back), a height chart, and a teenage girl's head sheet (two left profiles, a large neutral front, three-quarter smiling, high and low angles, rear views) with an eye-detail box at three levels of detail and red correction notes.
- **E7 [P, seen, negative]** <https://bookwalker.jp/de9a63d22f-1781-496b-b788-b3a85fb165fa/>. A second book's sample: the first six spreads are contents and episode stills; no character sheet reached.

By channel:

- **Official series sites** (E1–E3). Typical content is a coloured front bust and one full-body figure, front or three-quarter. Profile heads appear only where the site reproduces the sheet itself, and in E1 only for leads. Adult women appear as busts and full-body figures.
- **Official social accounts.** [P] A news page reports three designs of one character released the day after an episode aired in 2025, credited to the rights holders, without naming the channel: <https://www.oricon.co.jp/news/2391419/full/>. I opened no official post. Not verified.
- **Studio or broadcaster pages.** E4 is a studio release seen through the press. No studio site or broadcaster page was opened.
- **Online exhibitions and museums.** [S] Sheets are shown in physical venues (a municipal animation museum, a studio's museum); one film's online exhibition required registration; a university archive of production materials serves researchers. No open online gallery of 2015–2026 sheets was found.
- **Publisher previews** (E5–E7). Free, in the publisher's own viewer or shop page; whether the sample reaches the character pages varies by book.
- **Press kits.** E4. Also [P] <https://anime.eiga.com/news/125381/>: line-art sheets of three characters of a film, with the designer's notes; captions mention expressions, not views. Images not seen at readable size.
- **Free still programmes.** [P] Studio Ghibli's programme began on 2020-09-18 with 50 scene stills each from eight films and offers stills only, no sheets: <https://www.ghibli.jp/info/013344/>. [S] Another film studio opened stills from five films in 2021 on similar terms. Film stills are neither level nor in a stated view.

Inference: the efficient route is by work, not by search. Open the official character page and look for a close-up block; open the publisher's page for the work's setting-material book and try the sample. Five generic Japanese searches for published sheets returned announcements that never said which views were shown. In this sample three of seven sources gave all three views of one character and three gave no head sheet at all (E2, E3, E7), so the owner's cost is several pages opened per usable set, each head still to be judged by eye.

## 2. Books and paid material

- [P] Mainstream publisher: AB format, 144 pages, 2,970 yen, 2018, supervised by the production committee, paper and e-book, free sample on the publisher's e-book store (E5). A second: B5, 160 pages, 3,080 yen, 2022; the description lists an episode guide, character sheets, props and art: <https://www.kadokawa.co.jp/product/322108000960/>.
- [P] Specialist publisher: B5, 296 pages, full colour, 4,000 yen plus tax; line-art sheets (character design, art, props), art boards, storyboard excerpts, the chief animation director's corrections and drawing notes; sold first at a fan convention, then through bookshops, a large online retailer and the publisher's shop, which showed it sold out with a second order round: <https://animeanime.jp/release/prtimes/20251219/263397.html>, E6.
- [S] Studio-run shops sell their own collections, for example a two-volume character and art set at 11,000 yen with an electronic edition, and a 108-page film collection at 4,000 yen.
- [S] Blu-ray booklets are commonly 32 to 48 pages of interviews, character introductions and art settings; contents vary by title. None was verified to contain head turnarounds.
- Inference from E5 and E6: for a lead, a book gives a neutral front head, a profile, three-quarter heads with expressions, high and low angles and rear views, plus a full-body row drawn at one scale on one ground line. Books are where profile heads of secondary and adult characters would be; I verified that for no adult woman.
- Terms: [P] the two product pages state nothing on reuse, analysis or AI; they carry a copyright line, the mark for licensed e-book services and a link to a policy page I did not open. [P] The specialist shop page shows no terms. No publisher statement on analysis was found.

## 3. Terms

- [P] Studio Ghibli's stills page gives one condition: "常識の範囲でご自由にお使いください". Nothing on analysis.
- [P] The portal behind E1 (operator: the series' production company; revised 2025-10-01) treats use of the site as consent to its terms; forbids copying, distributing, adapting or reusing its content without permission; forbids altering it; and forbids reposting beyond lawful quotation, for profit or not. It has no clause on scraping, analysis or AI: <https://gundam-official.com/terms>.
- [P] A news site that relays press images forbids unauthorised reposting in its footer (animeanime.jp).
- [P] Japan: Article 30-4 permits exploiting a work to the extent necessary where the purpose is not enjoyment of what it expresses, naming data analysis (statistical analysis of images and other elements from many works), unless the copyright owner's interests are unreasonably prejudiced. The Agency for Cultural Affairs' overview (May 2024) says collecting from sites known to distribute pirated or infringing content should be strictly avoided, and that the document is not legally binding: <https://www.bunka.go.jp/english/policy/copyright/pdf/94055801_01.pdf>.
- [P] EU: Directive 2019/790 defines text and data mining as automated analysis of digital text and data to generate information. Article 3 covers research organisations and cultural heritage institutions mining for scientific research with lawful access. Article 4 covers anyone, for lawfully accessible works, with copies kept only as long as the mining needs, and only where the rightholder has not expressly reserved the use, by machine-readable means for online content: <https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32019L0790>.
- [P] US: no statutory exception; 17 U.S.C. §107 weighs the purpose and character of the use, the nature of the work, the amount used and the effect on the market: <https://www.copyright.gov/title17/92chap1.html>.
- Inference: measuring on screen and keeping only numbers involves the least copying under all three. A saved copy sits inside the Japanese exception's wording but against the letter of a site's no-copying terms; whether such terms override Article 30-4 was not researched, here or in the 2026-11-15 legal note.

## 4. Fan and scan aggregators (recognise, do not collect)

None was opened. Kinds, from the 2026-11-15 notes [S] and Inference:

- Tag-indexed image boards carrying fan uploads tagged as reference sheets, turnarounds or production art; boards devoted to animation clips and production material.
- Blogs, galleries and shops built around scanned sheets, and file hosts or archives holding whole scanned books.
- Fan wikis with design galleries; reposts on social media and pin boards without a rights-holder account.
- Resale and auction listings photographing studio photocopies: the object may be lawfully resold, the listing photo is not a licensed publication.
- [S] Pages of machine-generated "turnaround sheets". They dominated my English search results and are not production drawings.

Signs of a lawful source: the rights holder's own domain or a named publisher or shop, a production-committee copyright line, and a product behind the sample.

## 5. Telling a level head from a tilted or turned one

- [P] Low angle: the eye line sits above the head's middle parallel and bows; ears drop relative to the face; the area under the chin widens; the underside of the nose shows; brows and crown compress. High angle: the eye line sits below the middle parallel. <https://medibangpaint.com/en/use/2020/12/how-to-draw-various-face-directions-aori>
- [P] On an angled face the far eye is partly hidden rather than merely narrower, the lower lids splay, and the underside of the chin can show: <https://www.clipstudio.net/oekaki/archives/153458>.
- [P] A portrait lesson reads tilt from the line joining ear and brow (it points up when the head tilts up, down when it tilts down) and reads turn by comparing the width of the front plane with the side plane: <https://www.proko.com/s/1cX2>.
- [S] Tutorials after Loomis place the ear between the brow line and the nose line on a level head; it drops below when the head tilts up and rises when it tilts down.
- [P] A published method without a trained model (Gee and Cipolla, 1994, as described by Sapienza and Camilleri): roll from the angle of the line through the eyes; the symmetry axis from the midpoint between the eyes to the mouth; the nose base placed on that axis by a fixed ratio; the facial normal from nose base to nose tip, giving slant. Reported mean errors on video were about 3° roll, 5° yaw and 4° pitch, within about ±35°: <https://www.um.edu.mt/library/oar/bitstream/123456789/859/3/SCE-TR-2014-01.pdf>.
- Inference, untested:
  - Roll: the line through the two eye centres is horizontal.
  - Front: the midline (between the eyes, nose, mouth, chin) lies midway between the skull outlines at eye level. With half-width r and offset d, yaw ≈ arcsin(d / r); both ears show equally.
  - Level: the brow and nose-base lines are straight; the ear spans roughly brow to nose base; no underside of chin or nose and no extra crown.
  - Pitch from a sheet: pitching the head moves the face lines against the ear by about ρ·sin(pitch), where ρ is the ear-to-face distance read from the same character's profile. This is one way a profile ties depth to the front view.
  - Profile: one eye, no far cheek or brow, nose tip and chin on the silhouette.
  - Three-quarter is not a fixed angle; measure yaw per drawing.
  - Limits: anime noses are too slight for the nose-normal method, hair hides the skull outline, and these drawings are not strict projections of one solid, so any angle is nominal.

## 6. How production sheets mark views

- [P, seen] In E1 the labels FRONT, BACK and CLOSE UP belong to the web page; in E5 the labels 全身 and 表情 belong to the book layout. Neither labels individual head views.
- [P, seen] In E6 the sheets carry boxed typed notes (costume, detail level, episode ranges) and red corrections. At the size shown (a 900-pixel spread) I saw no per-view labels and no horizontal guides across the heads. Ruled horizontal lines appear only on the height chart, a centimetre scale from 90 to 180.
- [P, seen] Full-body rows in E5 and E6 stand on one ground line at one scale, so crown, eye and chin heights can be carried across views with a horizontal rule. Head sheets place heads at mixed sizes and positions and must be normalised by head height.
- Inference: view is read from the drawing and its place in the row, not from a label; the square grid behind E1's figures is page decoration.
